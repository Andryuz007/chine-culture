The Spring Festival

This is an important tradition in China. It is a very colorful celebration with dragons and red lanterns.

Grammar Focus: Articles (a, an, the)

Explore the Traditions

1. The Festival Preparation

+

Before the festival, everyone cleans the house to remove bad luck. Someone puts red decorations and lanterns on the doors. Everyone wears new clothes, usually in red.

Grammar Focus: Indefinite Pronouns (everyone, someone, nobody).

2. Culture and Traditions

+

On New Year's Eve, families prepare a big dinner. Nobody sleeps early because they stay up to watch fireworks and give red envelopes with money to children. Even if they are tired, they work through the night.

People celebrate it with their families every year. A family is a group who supports you. This dinner is an event that brings people together.

Grammar Focus: Phrasal Verbs (work through) & Defining Relative Clauses (who, that).

3. My Experience

+

I want to share my experience. I had never understood why red is so important in China. Chinese people had celebrated this festival for thousands of years before it became famous worldwide.

Grammar Focus: Past Perfect (had + past participle).
