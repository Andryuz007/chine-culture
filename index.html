The Spring Festival

This is an important tradition in China. It is an extremely colorful celebration with dragons and red lanterns.

🏮 🏮 🏮

Explore the Traditions

1. The Festival Preparation

+
  • Everyone cleans the house (remove bad luck).
  • Someone puts red decorations on doors.
  • Everyone wears new red clothes.

2. Culture and Traditions

+
  • New Year's Eve: Big family dinner.
  • Nobody sleeps early. They stay up for fireworks.
  • Children receive money in red envelopes.
  • A family is a group who supports you. This event that brings people together. They enjoy sharing food.

3. A Universal Connection (Colombia)

+

I think it's interesting because cleaning is a universal way to start a new year.

4. My Research Experience

+
  • The community started this tradition centuries ago.
  • During my research, I had never understood the importance of red until I read about it.
  • I learned they had celebrated it long before it became global.
  • It is important to respect these customs.
